# Glossary Map

## Contexts

- [Process Panel](./cases/process-panel/GLOSSARY.md): a local page that shows running dev projects and stops forgotten ones
- [Support agent](./cases/support-agent/GLOSSARY.md): first-line support assistant for a home internet and TV provider

## Relationships

- **Support agent ↔ company systems**: the systems of the company are outside the assistant's context; their words are translated into the assistant's words at the border (`cases/support-agent/system-words.md`).
